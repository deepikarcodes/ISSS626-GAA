1 1. Setting the Scene

Housing is an essential component of household wealth worldwide. Purchasing a home has always represented a major investment for most individuals. The price of housing is influenced by a wide range of factors including structural characteristics like size and fittings, as well as locational factors such as proximity to amenities and transportation.

Traditional housing resale price models using Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) often fail to account for spatial autocorrelation and spatial heterogeneity present in geographic datasets. Geographically Weighted Models (GWMs) address these limitations by allowing relationships to vary across space, providing more accurate and localized insights.

1.1 1.a. Objectives

This exercise applies geographically weighted methods to:

  • Identify and evaluate factors influencing HDB resale prices during January to September 2025

  • Account for spatial heterogeneity in housing price determinants across Singapore

  • Provide localized insights for housing market understanding and policy planning

1.2 1.b. Analysis Questions

  • What are the key structural and locational factors affecting HDB resale prices?

  • How do these relationships vary spatially across different neighborhoods?

  • Where are the areas with strongest/weakest price determinants?

  • What are the implications for housing market participants and urban planners?

  data = train_data,
